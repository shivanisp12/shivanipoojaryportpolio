About

Rigour with the people behind the numbers

I’m an MBA graduate in Finance and Analytics. My hands-on work so far sits in two places: assessing auto-loan applications at Kanchana Hyundai, and valuing equity and index options on NIFTY 50 stocks.

I enjoy problems where documentation, affordability and risk must be reconciled into a clear call, and where a statistical test can settle what intuition only suggests. I have also led teams in community and CSR programmes.
